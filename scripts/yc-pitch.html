Expert Capture Pipeline

One video in, structured
knowledge out.

The expert capture pipeline turns a raw walkthrough video into training-quality documentation in 3-8 minutes — work that previously took weeks of technical writing.

1

Video Upload

Expert records a walkthrough showing the procedure — any camera, any format (MP4, WebM, MOV, MPEG). Up to 500MB. Uploaded to Vercel Blob.

2

Visual + Audio Analysis

Gemini 2.0 Flash watches every frame and listens to the narration. Extracts scenes, procedures, equipment, safety hazards, and timestamps.

3

Knowledge Synthesis

Claude Sonnet 4.5 synthesizes the raw analysis into a clean ExpertKnowledgeDocument: step-by-step procedures with exact specs (torque values, temperatures, pressures, part numbers).

4

Semantic Indexing

Pre-chunked by procedure step, Q&A pair, and safety note. Embedded with text-embedding-3-small (1536-dim vectors). Stored in PostgreSQL + pgvector.

5

Instantly Available

Procedure appears in the Guide Library. Workers can start following it immediately with voice guidance, or search it via RAG chat.

What the AI extracts

  • Procedures — numbered steps with titles, descriptions, safety levels, timestamps
  • Equipment — tools, machines, parts with part numbers and specs
  • Safety notes — PPE, hazards, procedures, environmental warnings
  • Q&A pairs — 8-20 realistic questions covering how-to, safety, troubleshooting, common mistakes
  • Full transcript — complete audio transcription for searchability
  • Executive summary — 2-3 paragraph overview of the procedure

Safety-aware by design

  • CRITICAL — red badge, TTS prefixes "Warning. Critical safety step."
  • WARNING — yellow badge, TTS prefixes "Caution."
  • Auto-escalated for electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, rotating, and chemical hazards
  • Warnings displayed in highlighted callout boxes on each step

Step-by-Step Guidance

The worker experience:
fully hands-free.

Worker opens a guide on their phone. Each step is read aloud. They say "next" when done. If confused, they ask a question and hear the answer. No hands needed.

1

Browse & start a guide

Worker opens the Guide Library, sees available procedures with step counts, safety badges, and expert names. Taps "Start Guide" or "Resume" to continue from where they left off.

2

Step-by-step with auto-play audio

Each step shows a progress bar, title, description, safety level, and warnings. The step description is automatically read aloud via TTS. Safety steps are prefixed with voice warnings.

3

Voice commands — no hands needed

Worker says "next" when done, "repeat" to hear it again, "pause" to stop audio, "go back" to revisit. Mic pauses during TTS to prevent feedback, then auto-resumes.

4

Ask anything mid-step

Any spoken phrase that isn't a command becomes a question. It goes to the contextual AI chat, gets a RAG-powered answer specific to this step, and the response is read aloud automatically.

5

Complete & track progress

On the last step, worker says "done" or taps "Complete". Session is logged. Managers can see procedure completion status. Workers can resume any procedure where they left off.

The hands-free loop

1. Step is read aloud
2. Worker performs the step
3. Says "next" → advances
OR Asks a question → AI answers aloud
4. Mic auto-resumes after TTS
5. Repeat until complete
